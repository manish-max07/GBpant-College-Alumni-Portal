# 🎓 GB Pant Engineering College Alumni Portal

Welcome to the **GB Pant Alumni Portal**—a premium, modern, and interactive networking platform designed to connect generations of graduates and current students of GB Pant Engineering College (GBPEC). 

This platform bridges the gap between college life and the professional world, facilitating seamless networking, career mentorship, peer discovery, and professional collaboration.

---

## 🌟 What You Will See (User Experience)

The platform is designed with a sleek, glassmorphic, and fully responsive user interface. Whether you are an alumni returning to give back or a current student seeking guidance, you'll experience:

*   **A Personalized Dashboard**: A clean, unified overview of your profile status, network metrics, and key actions.
*   **An Interactive Network Hub**: A searchable database of members with filter tags for programs, graduation years, companies, and roles.
*   **A Modern, Secure Auth Flow**: Easy signups, security-enhanced OTP logins, and secure account recovery options.

---

## 🛠️ Features Breakdown

### 💼 For Alumni (Giving Back)
As an alumnus, your profile serves as a professional beacon for the college community:
*   **Professional Profiles**: Showcase your career journey, including current company, job role, industry, higher education background, and social media links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio).
*   **Mentorship Channels**: Make yourself discoverable to current students who need career guidance, internship advice, or interview prep.
*   **Network & Reconnect**: Search and find fellow batchmates, explore where your peers are working globally, and foster professional partnerships.
*   **Verification Status**: Profiles go through verification to maintain an authentic, high-trust network of real college graduates.

### 🎓 For Students (Learning & Growing)
As a current student, the portal acts as a direct link to industry professionals:
*   **Find Mentors**: Browse profiles of seniors working in top tech companies, startups, research institutes, or pursuing higher education globally.
*   **Advanced Search & Filter**: Easily filter the alumni directory by engineering branch (CSE, ECE, ME, etc.), degree program (B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA), company name, or graduation year.
*   **Direct Contact**: Securely access professional details and social profiles shared by alumni to reach out for guidance.

### 🛡️ Core Platform Security & Tech Stack
The portal operates on a robust, state-of-the-art security foundation to protect your data:
*   **Anti-Spam CAPTCHA**: Prevents bots from registering fake profiles.
*   **Email Domain Validation**: Blocks temporary, suspicious, or throwaway email addresses to ensure genuine signups.
*   **IP-Based Rate Limiting**: Protects login and signup endpoints from brute-force attempts.
*   **Tech Stack**: Built using a modern stack consisting of:
    *   **Frontend**: React, Tailwind CSS, Vite
    *   **Backend**: Node.js, Express
    *   **Database**: PostgreSQL (Supabase)
    *   **Hosting**: Render (Frontend static site), Vercel (Backend API)

## 🚀 How to Get Started

### Sign Up as a Member
1.  Go to the signup page.
2.  Choose whether you are a **Student** or **Alumnus**.
3.  Enter your details (students require a valid college roll number).
4.  Verify your email using the secure OTP system.
5.  Set up your password and complete your profile card!
